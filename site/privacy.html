Privacy Policy

Effective date: 12 November 2026

In short: on this website we collect your email address to keep you updated about Navoo, plus basic information about how you found us. In the Navoo app we collect what you choose to share so the AI coach can support you; most of it stays on your phone, and you can export or delete it at any time. We use advertising and analytics tools (Meta, Google) to measure our ads. We don’t sell your personal information, and you can ask us to delete it at any time.

This Privacy Policy explains how {{COMPANY}} (“Navoo”, “we”, “us”) collects and uses personal information when you visit this website, join the Navoo waitlist or use the Navoo app. Sections 1 to 4 cover this website; section 5 covers the app.

2. How we use your information

  • Launch updates. We email you about Navoo’s launch, early access and helpful content. Every email lets you unsubscribe.
  • Measuring and improving. We look at sign-ups, answers and traffic sources in aggregate to improve the website and our future product.
  • Advertising. We tell advertising platforms when someone signs up so they can measure our ads and show them to people likely to be interested.
  • Security. We use basic signals (for example, rate limits and spam checks) to protect the site from abuse.

3. Legal basis (UK and EU visitors)

We process your information because you asked us to (add you to the waitlist), on the basis of your consent for marketing emails and non-essential cookies, and for our legitimate interests in measuring and improving our marketing. You can withdraw consent at any time.

4. Cookies and tracking

We use the following technologies, where enabled:

  • Meta Pixel: records page views and sign-ups so Meta can measure our ads. It sets cookies such as _fbp.
  • Meta Conversions API: when you sign up we send Meta your email address in hashed (scrambled, irreversible) form, together with your IP address, browser user agent and Meta cookie IDs, so Meta can match the sign-up to an ad click.
  • Google Tag Manager, Google Analytics and Google Ads: measure traffic and conversions, and may set cookies such as _ga.
  • Your browser’s session storage: remembers where you came from while the tab is open. It’s cleared when you close the tab.

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, opt out of personalised ads in your Facebook ad preferences and Google ad settings, and use the Google Analytics opt-out.

5. The Navoo app

This section applies when you use the Navoo app.

What we collect

When you use the Navoo app we collect:

  • your name, email and password (stored securely by our login provider, Supabase);
  • what you share during setup: your vision, 30-day focus, obstacles, motivations and reward;
  • your actions, check-ins and how they felt;
  • your conversations with the Navoo coach;
  • anything optional you add, such as weight, height, activity level or a profile photo.

Most of this is stored on your phone.

AI coaching

Your messages and relevant parts of your profile are sent to Google’s Gemini AI to generate the coach’s replies, suggestions and summaries. The coach learns from your conversations to personalise future support; you can turn this off or clear what it has learned in Me → Privacy & AI. AI replies are not medical advice.

Health information

Weight and wellbeing details are sensitive. We use them only to coach you, never for advertising, and we never sell them.

Safety

If you mention self-harm, suicide or signs of an eating disorder, Navoo shows support resources and sends a private alert (your email, the type of concern, the time and a short excerpt of your message) to a member of the Navoo team so they can follow up appropriately.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled on your phone; you can change or turn them off in Settings.

Your control

You can export your data or permanently delete your account and data at any time in Me → Privacy & AI.

8. How long we keep it

We keep your waitlist sign-up until Navoo launches and for up to 24 months afterwards, or until you ask us to delete it or unsubscribe, whichever is sooner. App data is kept until you delete it or delete your account in Me → Privacy & AI. Analytics and advertising data is kept by Meta and Google according to their own retention settings.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the UK GDPR, EU GDPR, India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023, Australian Privacy Principles or US state privacy laws), you may have the right to:

  • access the personal information we hold about you and get a copy;
  • correct it or ask us to delete it;
  • object to or restrict how we use it, including for direct marketing;
  • withdraw your consent at any time;
  • complain to your local data protection authority.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us {{CONTACT}}. We’ll respond within 30 days.

10. Children

Navoo is intended for adults aged 18 and over. We don’t knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we’ll delete it.

11. Security

We use reputable providers, encrypted connections (HTTPS) and access controls to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work to protect your information and limit who can access it.

12. Health information

Navoo provides general wellbeing guidance and is not medical advice. See “Health information” in the app section above for how we treat weight and wellbeing details. If you have a health condition, please speak to a healthcare professional before making changes.

We may update this policy from time to time. We’ll change the effective date above, and if the changes are significant we’ll let you know by email.
